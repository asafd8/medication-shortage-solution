# Medication Shortage Solution

> Exploring accessible, regulated solutions to medication shortages — 
> because no patient should face a dead end.

## 🩺 The Problem

People with chronic illnesses often face:
- **Stock-outs** — medications unavailable at pharmacies
- **Logistical failures** — partial, delayed, or lost deliveries
- **Discontinued production** — drugs abandoned for economic reasons
- **Running between pharmacies** — a burden on patients and families

For a patient, this isn't an inconvenience. It's a dead end.

## 💡 The Idea

Exploring accessible, regulated solutions — potentially including 
decentralized manufacturing technologies — to ensure patients have 
reliable access to essential medications.

**Any implementation would require full coordination with health 
authorities and regulatory approval. This is a concept and research 
project, not a medical or pharmaceutical service.**
